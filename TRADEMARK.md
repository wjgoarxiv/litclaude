# Trademark policy

The code in this repository is free to use under the MIT License (see `LICENSE`). The names **LitClaude**
and **LitFamily**, and the logos and mascot art, are not part of that license.

- You can fork, change and share the code under MIT.
- If you publish a changed version, give it a different name and logo, and do not suggest that the
  LitClaude maintainers made or endorse it.
- You can say in plain text that your project is "based on LitClaude" or "a fork of LitClaude".
- Unchanged copies, such as mirrors and package caches, may keep the name.

Questions: open an issue at https://github.com/wjgoarxiv/litclaude/issues.
