# Summary of changes

From Saturday 1 November, Building B moves to badge-only entry at all four doors (main, east, loading and garage), visitor parking moves from the front lot to level 1 of the north garage, and the night entrance after 22:00 becomes the east door only. Nothing changes for staff who already badge in at the main door. Visitors, contractors and late shifts are the groups most affected, and this memo lists what each of them needs to do before the end of October.

# Reasons for the change

Three incidents in the last quarter involved people entering Building B through the loading door without a badge, and the insurer has asked for badge records at every external door by the end of the year. The front lot is also needed for the fire service access route that the city approved in September. Moving visitor parking to level 1 of the north garage gives 60 spaces against the 38 in the front lot (sample figures), and the garage already has a covered walkway to reception.

# Actions by group

## Staff on day shifts

No action is needed if your badge already opens the main door. Check that it also opens the east door before 1 November; the security desk can update a badge in about two minutes. Badges issued before 2022 use the older card type and must be replaced, which takes one working day.

## Visitors and hosts

Register every visitor arriving after 1 November in the visitor system at least one working day ahead. The invitation email now includes a garage code for level 1, valid on the day of the visit only. Visitors without a registration will be asked to wait at the garage reception desk while security calls the host, which usually takes ten to fifteen minutes and has caused missed meetings in the pilot week.

The access road in front of reception becomes a fire lane on 1 November. Do not ask visitors to wait there, even for a few minutes; security will ask any parked vehicle to move.

## Contractors

Contractors receive a temporary badge at the loading door office for the length of their work order. A contractor badge stops working at the end date on the order, so hosts should extend the order in the facilities system rather than lend a staff badge. Lending a badge is now a reportable security incident.

## Late and night shifts

After 22:00 only the east door opens. The main door stays locked from the inside as well, except as a fire exit. The east door has a staffed desk until 02:00 and a call button to the control room after that.
